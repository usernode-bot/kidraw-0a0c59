# Kidraw

A playful drawing board with crayons and stamps.

Open it to a blank sheet of paper and draw with a finger, a mouse or a
stylus. Pick one of seven crayons, draw freehand with the brush or drop a
star or heart stamp, and choose one of four sizes. Undo takes back the last
line or stamp; Clear wipes the sheet, and Undo brings the whole drawing back
if that was a mistake.

**Save picture** saves the drawing to the device as a PNG on white paper.
On a phone it opens the device's own share sheet; elsewhere it downloads the
file.

Nothing is stored on the server: a drawing lives in the open page and leaves
as the picture you save. Sign-in, the platform bridge and the app's private
Postgres are the Homeroom scaffold's plumbing, unchanged.
